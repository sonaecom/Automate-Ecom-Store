# Homepage asset sizes

Policy preflight: read `{baseDir}/policies/LAYER.md` and run `policy-gate.mjs` for this platform and market before any action in this file.

Dry run follows `{baseDir}/modules/ops/dry-run.md`: walk the steps, take the screenshots, write the report, and do not click Save, Submit, or Send.

The editor on the day wins. If the crop tool or the component panel shows a different pixel size, file-size limit, or ratio, use that and do not upload the file from this table.

"Verify" means the figure comes from a seller-education page or a widely repeated guide, and the live editor can still differ by country or by component. Read the number in the panel before you export.

File format unless the panel says otherwise: JPG, JPEG, or PNG. Do not put a price on a banner unless the plan names that exact live price.

## Shopee Shop Decoration

Source: Shopee Seller Education, Shop Decoration component guidelines (SG, article dated 2024-04-07; MY carousel ratios). Logo size is from an older Shop Information guide and must be verified.

| Asset | Draft size | File limit | Verify in the editor |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Shop cover | 1200 × 675 px | 2 MB | Yes. Some markets still show 1200 × 600. |
| Carousel, ratio 16:9 | 2000 × 1125 px | 2 MB | Yes. Choose the ratio in the panel first. |
| Carousel, ratio 2:1 | 2000 × 1000 px | 2 MB | Yes. Same panel. |
| Carousel, ratio 1:1 | 2000 × 2000 px | 2 MB | Yes. SG also describes a 2000 × 2000 upload that is then cropped to the selected ratio. |
| Carousel and category | width 1200 px, height 100–2200 px | 2 MB | Yes. The first image sets the ratio. |
| Banner and category | width 1200 px, height 100–900 px | 2 MB | Yes. The first image sets the ratio. |
| Shop logo | square, often 300 × 300 px | 2 MB | Yes. Read Shop Information. |

Up to 6 images or videos in one carousel. JPG, JPEG, PNG. GIF is allowed on the carousel in the SG guide. Keep type inside the centre so a crop does not cut the words.

## TikTok Shop page

Source: TikTok Shop Seller University, Shop Page Design (component table) and the US Shop Design header note. Blog sizes such as 1440 × 480 or 1500 × 500 are not the spec. Do not use them.

| Asset | Draft size | File limit | Verify in the editor |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Single image, hero | 16:9, width at least 800 px. Draft 1600 × 900. | 5 MB | Yes. The panel states the ratio. |
| Multi-click image | 1200 × 675 px recommended, width greater than 800 | 2 MB | Yes. |
| Multi-image tile | 800 × 800 px recommended | 2 MB | Yes. 2–4 pictures. |
| Collection image | width greater than 800 px | 2 MB | Yes. |
| Header background | 780 × 720 px | 2 MB | Yes. Shown in the US header guide. Your market may hide this slot. |
| Logo | PNG or WEBP, on a white or transparent ground | 2 MB | Yes. |

Carousel ratios in the panel include 8:10, 10:8, and free. Pick the ratio in the panel, then export that ratio. JPG, JPEG, PNG.

## Lazada Store Builder

Source: repeated Store Builder guides for the header and logo. Module banner sizes are from a third-party decoration note, not a Lazada help page. Treat every Lazada row as verify-in-app. The crop tool after upload is the real spec.

| Asset | Draft size | File limit | Verify in the editor |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Header, PC | 1200 × 128 px | 1 MB until the tool says otherwise | Yes. |
| Header, mobile | 750 × 180 px | 1 MB until the tool says otherwise | Yes. |
| Store logo | 600 × 600 px | 1 MB until the tool says otherwise | Yes. |
| Single banner module | 1200 × 50 px is a published suggestion | 1 MB | Yes. Easy to get wrong. Read the module. |
| Carousel or multi-click banner | width 1200 or 1920 px | 1 MB | Yes. Height comes from the module. |
| Multiple-image banner | 600 × 300 px | 1 MB | Yes. |
| Four-image tile | about 597 × 597 px | 1 MB | Yes. |
| Five-image tile | about 292 × 292 px | 1 MB | Yes. |

Prepare both the PC header and the mobile header. They are not the same picture scaled down.
