# Competitor monitoring

Policy preflight: read `{baseDir}/policies/LAYER.md` and run `policy-gate.mjs` for this platform and market before any action in this file.

Dry run follows `{baseDir}/modules/ops/dry-run.md`: walk the steps, take the screenshots, write the report, and do not click Save, Submit, or Send.

Read public listing pages in a browser that is not the seller profile. Record price, rating count, and ship promise when the page shows them. Alert when a figure changed since the last read. Do not copy their photos, title, or brand. Do not change our price from this file.

Policy gate action is `read`.

## When to use

The owner wants a repeated look at the public listings they already named. A one-time scorecard is `{baseDir}/modules/growth/scorecard.md`.

## Steps

1. Use the non-store browser. Open only the URLs or search terms in the private watch list. Labels stay `<COMPETITOR_1>` and so on.
2. Copy the figures that are on the page. If a figure is missing, leave it blank. Do not estimate.
3. Compare with the previous CSV. A price or a rating-count change is an alert with both numbers. Do not average two reads that disagree. Show both.
4. A price move on our side is a proposal in `{baseDir}/modules/listing/market-price.md`. Do not type it here.
5. One pass per session. Do not refresh the same page in a loop.

## QC

Every alert has the old figure, the new figure, and the read time. The seller profile was not used.

## Stop

You would open the competitor inside the seller profile. You would copy their images into our listing. You would change our price to match them without a yes.

## Report

How many listings were read, which figures changed, both numbers, read time.
