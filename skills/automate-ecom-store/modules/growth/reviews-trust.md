# Early reviews and trust

Policy preflight: read `{baseDir}/policies/LAYER.md` and run `policy-gate.mjs` for this platform and market before any action in this file.

Dry run follows `{baseDir}/modules/ops/dry-run.md`: walk the steps, take the screenshots, write the report, and do not click Save, Submit, or Send.

Use only the platform's own tools. No bought reviews, no fake orders, no star target.

Policy gate for reading the tools is `read`. Turning on a platform review reward is `publish` and needs `ALLOW` plus the owner's yes for that budget of coins or vouchers.

## When to use

A new shop needs trust on the page before ads spend.

## Steps

1. Read `{baseDir}/modules/customers/post-delivery.md` before any buyer message. That file is the only chat ask. This module does not send it.
2. Check the public shop page from a non-store browser: logo, cover, shipping promise, and response settings that are already visible. Missing items become a fix list. Do not invent a badge the page does not offer.
3. Shopee: the official tool is Review Prize in Marketing Centre. Coins go to reviews that meet Shopee's quality rules. The seller does not pick the reviews, and the reward is not tied to a star count. Cite [Shopee Review Prize](https://seller.shopee.sg/edu/article/13356/review-prize). Propose the coin amount from the owner's note. If the note has no amount, ask. Do not turn it on without a yes.
4. TikTok Shop: use only the in-app incentivized review tool when that shop's Seller Center shows it. The reward, if the tool allows one, is for a review that meets the tool's length and photo rules, and it is paid for any star rating. Do not offer a reward in chat. Cite the [TikTok Shop review policy](https://seller-us.tiktok.com/university/essay?knowledge_id=4451321470682882&lang=en).
5. Lazada: use a review reward only when Seller Center shows an official control for it. If it is not on screen, skip the reward. Do not copy another platform's coin tool.
6. Never pay for a review, a follower, or an order. Brushing is forbidden. Cite [Shopee's anti-brushing policy](https://seller.shopee.sg/edu/article/11697/anti-brushing-policy).

## QC

The proposal matches the on-screen tool, including that the seller cannot choose a 5-star subset. Two checks before save and on the saved promotion, and only after the owner said yes.

## Stop

The plan needs a fake order or a bought review. The reward would depend on the star rating. The owner has not said yes.

## Report

What is already on the public page, the tool proposed or saved, the budget of coins or vouchers, and the policy links you used.
