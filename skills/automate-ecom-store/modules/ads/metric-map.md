# Where ad spend and top-ups are read

Policy preflight: read `{baseDir}/policies/LAYER.md` and run `policy-gate.mjs` for this platform and market before any action in this file.

Dry run follows `{baseDir}/modules/ops/dry-run.md`: walk the steps, take the screenshots, write the report, and do not click Save, Submit, or Send.

Read the number on the screen or in the official export. The column header you see wins over this table. Record that header in `source`. Record `read_time` as the shop computer's local time. The `date` is the date on the report, in the timezone the report itself prints. Do not convert it. Do not click Top up, Add funds, Pay, or Withdraw.

## Shopee Ads, including GMV Max

| Item | Usual place | Name on screen | Date basis |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Spend | Marketing Centre, Shopee Ads, then the report for the campaign type. GMV Max has its own report. | Spend, Expense, or Ad expense. Confirm the header. | The report's own date, shop market local time as labeled. |
| Attributed GMV | Same report. | GMV. For GMV Max, Broad GMV if that is the column the report uses. | Same date as spend. |
| ROAS | Same report. | ROAS. | Same date. |
| Balance and top-up history | The ads credit or balance page in Shopee Ads. | Balance, Credit, Top-up history. | The history row's date. |

## TikTok Shop ads and GMV Max

| Item | Usual place | Name on screen | Date basis |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Spend | Seller Center, Ads or GMV Max, then the report. | Cost or Spend. Confirm the header. | The account timezone printed on the report. |
| Attributed GMV | Same report. | Gross revenue or GMV. | Same date as spend. |
| ROAS | Same report. | ROI or ROAS. Record which word you saw. | Same date. |
