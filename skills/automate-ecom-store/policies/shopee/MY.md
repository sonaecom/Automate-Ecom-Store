## Rules

- **[SH-MY-026] (fulfilment, warn)** MUST keep LSR below 10% — threshold: LSR exceeds 10% = 1 penalty point; +1 additional point if LSR exceeds 10% and ≥ 50 late-shipped orders — source: https://seller.shopee.com.my/edu/article/2153/high-late-shipment-rate-LSR-penalty-points-violation
- **[SH-MY-027] (fraud, block)** MUST NOT buy or attempt to buy from own shop (seller or staff), incl. to test checkout/vouchers — threshold: Penalty points under fraud for false transactions (points not stated in rendered text) — source: https://seller.shopee.com.my/edu/article/2082/calculate-seller-penalty-points-issued
- **[SH-MY-028] (fraud, block)** MUST NOT send empty parcels or irrelevant/lower-value items (Shipping Fraud) — source: https://seller.shopee.com.my/edu/article/2082/calculate-seller-penalty-points-issued
- **[SH-MY-029] (listing, block)** MUST NOT list switched listings, advertisements or misleading discounts — source: https://seller.shopee.com.my/edu/article/2082/calculate-seller-penalty-points-issued
- **[SH-MY-030] (listing, block)** MUST NOT post spam or duplicate listings — threshold: All spam listings will be removed — source: https://seller.shopee.com.my/edu/article/2082/calculate-seller-penalty-points-issued
- **[SH-MY-031] (ip, block)** MUST NOT list IP-infringing or counterfeit items — threshold: Listings will be deleted — source: https://seller.shopee.com.my/edu/article/2082/calculate-seller-penalty-points-issued
- **[SH-MY-032] (listing, block)** MUST NOT use vulgar words, platform names (e.g. 'Shopee') or brand/flagship/official keywords for non-official shops in shop name — source: https://seller.shopee.com.my/edu/article/2082/calculate-seller-penalty-points-issued
- **[SH-MY-033] (livestream, block)** MUST NOT show explicit/sensitive content, harassment or prohibited items on Shopee Live — source: https://seller.shopee.com.my/edu/article/2082/calculate-seller-penalty-points-issued
- **[SH-MY-034] (pricing, block)** MUST NOT raise original price before a sale to exaggerate discount; MUST hold brand/manufacturer/distributor price-change proof before increasing price — threshold: If listing banned, provide proof within 3 working days or listing may be permanently deleted — source: https://seller.shopee.com.my/edu/article/1553/misleading-discount-prohibited-listing-
- **[SH-MY-035] (penalty, warn)** Penalty points updated weekly on Mondays; appeal allowed up to 7 days before punishment period (from 20 April 2026) — source: https://seller.shopee.com.my/edu/article/2082/calculate-seller-penalty-points-issued
