## Rules

- **[TI-ID-080] (penalty, warn)** AHR milestones: <150 no campaigns/affiliate enrolment 7 days; <100 14-day restrictions incl. listing freeze; <50 shop deactivated 28 days; 0 permanent suspension. Points cleared every 90 calendar days — threshold: Formal warning 20 points before each threshold; listing-freeze at <100 "[Does not apply to ID]" — source: https://seller-sg.tiktok.com/university/essay?knowledge_id=7654182014322434
- **[TI-ID-081] (ip, block)** MUST NOT sell counterfeit or IP-infringing products — threshold: IP infringement: 5 AHR point deduction up to shop closure; Counterfeit: 15; Knockoff: up to 15 — source: https://seller-sg.tiktok.com/university/essay?knowledge_id=7654182014322434
- **[TI-ID-082] (listing, block)** MUST NOT list or ship prohibited products — threshold: 25 AHR point deduction up to shop closure — source: https://seller-sg.tiktok.com/university/essay?knowledge_id=7654182014322434
- **[TI-ID-083] (listing, block)** MUST NOT display false information in listings — threshold: Up to 20 AHR point deduction and product suspension — source: https://seller-sg.tiktok.com/university/essay?knowledge_id=7654182014322434
- **[TI-ID-084] (chat, warn)** MUST reply to buyer messages within 12 hours — threshold: Low 12-Hour Response Rate: up to 20 AHR point deduction — source: https://seller-sg.tiktok.com/university/essay?knowledge_id=7654182014322434
- **[TI-ID-085] (chat, block)** MUST NOT send inappropriate/prohibited customer communication — threshold: Up to 50 AHR point deduction — source: https://seller-sg.tiktok.com/university/essay?knowledge_id=7654182014322434
- **[TI-ID-086] (fulfilment, warn)** MUST keep seller-fault cancellation, late dispatch and seller-fault return rates low — threshold: SFCR: up to 25 / up to 15 AHR; LDR: up to 10 AHR; seller-fault return/refund: 5 AHR; SNAD: 5 AHR up to shop closure — source: https://seller-sg.tiktok.com/university/essay?knowledge_id=7654182014322434
- **[TI-ID-087] (content, block)** MUST NOT post reproduced content; MUST NOT promote inconsistent/prohibited products in videos/LIVE (linked creator violations can bring joint penalties) — threshold: Reproduced content: shop closure; prohibited promotion: 25 AHR; inconsistent promotion: 5 AHR — source: https://seller-sg.tiktok.com/university/essay?knowledge_id=7654182014322434
- **[TI-ID-088] (privacy, block)** MUST NOT redirect buyers off-platform or share phone/email/social handles/URLs in any seller-to-buyer communication, listing or fulfilment material — threshold: Buyer PII violations: account suspension and permanent deactivation — source: https://seller-sg.tiktok.com/university/essay?knowledge_id=7654182014322434
- **[TI-ID-089] (privacy, block)** MUST NOT solicit, reuse, retain or share buyer PII beyond the specific order; MUST delete/anonymise after return/dispute window — source: https://seller-sg.tiktok.com/university/essay?knowledge_id=7654182014322434
- **[TI-ID-090] (security, block)** MUST activate 2-Step Verification — threshold: Non-activation listed as ground to withhold balance — source: https://seller-sg.tiktok.com/university/essay?knowledge_id=7654182014322434
- **[TI-ID-091] (reviews, block)** MUST NOT solicit positive reviews or offer any reward (money, vouchers, free/discounted products, refunds) for positive/altered feedback, e.g. 'voucher if you give me 5 stars' — source: https://seller-id.tokopedia.com/university/essay?knowledge_id=1536982430189328&lang=en
- **[TI-ID-092] (reviews, block)** MUST NOT pressure buyers to change reviews or threaten refunds (e.g. 'No return and refund for 1–3 stars') — source: https://seller-id.tokopedia.com/university/essay?knowledge_id=1536982430189328&lang=en
- **[TI-ID-093] (chat, block)** MUST NOT send unsolicited marketing messages (spam) — threshold: Message block; chat restriction in severe cases — source: https://seller-id.tokopedia.com/university/essay?knowledge_id=1536982430189328&lang=en
- **[TI-ID-094] (chat, block)** MUST NOT send external links, QR codes, offline store addresses or personal contacts; MUST NOT process refunds off-platform or ask buyers to cancel — source: https://seller-id.tokopedia.com/university/essay?knowledge_id=1536982430189328&lang=en
- **[TI-ID-095] (automation, block)** MUST NOT tamper with Buyer Messages or use unauthorised plug-ins/third-party tools to access the platform — source: https://seller-id.tokopedia.com/university/essay?knowledge_id=1536982430189328&lang=en
