## Rules

- **[LA-PH-096] (penalty, warn)** 48 non-compliance points = account deactivation; max 16 points per 7 days (except 48-point issues); points reset 365 days after issue; appeal within 60 days (IPR 14 days) — threshold: 48 points — source: https://sellercenter.lazada.sg/helpcenter/s/faq/knowledge?questionId=1000145984 — ⚠️ UNVERIFIED for this market: read from Lazada SG help-centre snippet; PH/MY/ID page not readable
- **[LA-PH-097] (listing, block)** MUST NOT list prohibited/controlled products (e.g. adult, firearms, illicit drugs, illegal services, controlled products) — threshold: Locked product and up to 48 points (category-dependent: up to 2/6/48) — source: https://sellercenter.lazada.sg/helpcenter/s/faq/knowledge?questionId=1000145968 — ⚠️ UNVERIFIED for this market: SG snippet
- **[LA-PH-102] (returns, warn)** Buyer may return within 14 and/or 7 days of delivery (depending on product) for wrong/faulty/not-as-advertised/missing parts/doesn't fit — threshold: 14 days and/or 7 days — source: https://www.lazada.com.ph/terms-conditions/
- **[LA-PH-103] (listing, block)** MUST NOT upload Prohibited Material (IP-infringing, counterfeit, fraudulent, obscene, malware) — source: https://www.lazada.com.ph/terms-conditions/
- **[LA-PH-104] (automation, block)** MUST NOT attempt unauthorised access to or interfere with/disrupt the Platform — source: https://www.lazada.com.ph/terms-conditions/
